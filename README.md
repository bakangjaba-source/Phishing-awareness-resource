# Phishing Awareness Resource

## About
This resource was made to detect phishing scams. People usually fall victim to a cyberattack due to the inability to recognise the red flags. With this resource if they suspect anything dodgy sent to them they could use the resource to spot the scam.

## Who it's for
The resource is for everyone regardless of the age and intelligence as attackers target psychological habits rather than technical flaws on the device.

## Contents
* checklists/spotting-red-flags.md: uncovering potential hazards
* reporting/how-to-report-phishing.md: guidelines for reporting security threats
* references-phishing.md: reference materials for phishing awareness
 
## How to use it
1. **Screenshot the suspicious message:** Capture the text, sender details, and full headers/URLS.
2. **Compare against the checklist:** Check for common red flags such as urgency, suspicious links or domain mismatches.
3. **Do NOT interact:** Avoid clicking links, opening attachments, or replying to the sender.
4. **Verify independently:** Reach out to the organisation with details from their official site instead of the details from the suspicious message.
5. **Report the message:** Follow the steps in reporting/how-to-report-phishing.md
