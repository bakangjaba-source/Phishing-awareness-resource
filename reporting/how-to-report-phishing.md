# How to Report a Suspected Phishing Message

## Before You Report 
- Do not interact further: Avoid opening attachments and clicking links.
- Disconnect if compromised: If you suspect an attachment contained malware, immediately disconnect your device from the internet.
  
## Who to Report it To
- The Spoofed organisation: Report the message immediately and directly to the company being impersonated using their official address(e.g jarvis@microsft.com instead off jarvis@microsoft.com).
  
## What to Include in Your Report
- Suspicious URLs: Copy the exact destination link but do not visit it.
- Sender Information: Include the visible "From" address and "Reply-to" address specified.
  
## After You Report
- Delete: Delete the message permanently from your inbox and block the sender's contact.
- Monitor your account: Check your account for login logs for any unrecognised login attempts.
  
## If You Clicked or Shared Details
- Change Passwords Immediately: Reset the password on the targeted account as well as any other account using the same password.
- Notify Financial Institutions: If banking details were entered, freeze affected cards immediately and notify your bank's fraud department.
- Run an antivirus scan: If an attachment was downloaded or executed, run a full system scan with updated antivirus software.
